The decision by the UNESCO World Heritage Committee, meeting for its 48th session in Busan, adopts a significant boundary modification for the natural heritage property first inscribed in 2021, which originally covered four zones: Gochang, Boseong-Suncheon and Sinan in Jeolla provinces and Seocheon in South Chungcheong Province. The Phase 2 expansion incorporates crucial tidal flats in Yeosu, Goheung and Muan in South Jeolla Province, as well as Seosan in South Chungcheong Province. The World Heritage Committee recognized that the tidal flats satisfy the World Heritage criterion for the conservation of biodiversity and endangered species.
